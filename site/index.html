For them

A service that is on their side.

  • Links from friends come back watchable in minutes.
  • They follow the creators they like, and new videos arrive on their own.
  • Every suggestion says why it is there.
  • A no comes with a reason and a way to appeal.
For you

One decision at a time, from your lock screen.

  • Only the uncertain ones reach you. Approve or decline without opening anything.
  • Controls are in plain language, by child and by age.
  • Their viewing history stays on a machine in your house.
  • You can read every line of the code that decides.

What is not in the box

Autoplay

One video ends, and that is the end.

Infinite scroll

The day’s feed is short. Finishing it is the point.

A cloud

One SQLite file on your machine. No accounts, no telemetry.

Scoreboards

No hour counts or rankings on a kid’s screen.

Set it up this evening.

$ curl -fsSL https://eddyhq.app/install.sh | sh

Or docker compose up -d from the repository. Then add your kids and send each device its pairing link.
